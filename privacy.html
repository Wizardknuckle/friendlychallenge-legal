Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Toil, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Friendly Challenge mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Who We Are

Friendly Challenge is operated by Toil, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the State of Utah, United States.

For any privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us at: friendlychallengeteam@gmail.com

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, a display name you choose, and a username derived from that display name. Your email is used to authenticate your account. We also record the date you accepted these terms and the version you accepted, so that we have a record of your agreement.
  • Profile settings: Your avatar configuration (the icon and colours you choose), your profile visibility setting, and your notification preferences.
  • Activity and challenge data: When you use the App, we collect the information you enter, including the challenges you create or join, the activities you log (such as activity type, value, and date), and your progress toward challenge goals.
  • Messages and reactions: If you post messages on a challenge's encouragement board, or like or comment on an activity, we collect and store that content along with your display name.
  • Subscription status: Which subscription tier your account is on.

Information collected automatically

  • Device push token: If you enable push notifications, we collect a device-specific token that allows us to send notifications to your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.
  • Basic technical information: Standard information necessary for the App to function, handled through our service providers described below.

We do not collect your precise location, we do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking in the current version of the App, and we do not collect payment information.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide the App's core features, including creating and joining challenges, logging activity, displaying leaderboards, and connecting you with friends
  • Send you notifications related to your challenges and account (if you enable them)
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App
  • Respond to your questions and support requests

Information Visible to Other Users

Friendly Challenge is a social app, so some of your information is visible to other people who use it. Please read this section carefully.

  • Activities, progress and board messages are visible to other people using the App. The App shows them to the participants of the challenge they belong to, but they are not technically restricted to those participants — any signed-in user of the App is able to access them. Please treat anything you log or post as visible to other users of Friendly Challenge, not only to the people in your challenge.
  • Your public profile is visible to other users. It consists of your display name, your username and your avatar. Other users can find you by searching for your name or username, unless you turn on the private profile setting.
  • Friend requests and challenge invitations include your display name and your email address, and are visible to the person you send them to.

Your email address, notification settings, subscription status and push notification tokens are not visible to other users. Those are readable only by your own account.

Please only share information you are comfortable making visible to other people using the App.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Google Firebase: We use Google Firebase (provided by Google LLC) to operate the App, including authentication, database storage (Cloud Firestore), server-side functions, and push notification delivery. Your information is stored on and processed through Google's infrastructure on our behalf. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy terms.
  • Expo: We use Expo's push notification service (provided by Expo, Inc.) to send notifications. When we send you a notification, your device push token and the content of that notification pass through Expo's infrastructure before reaching Google's and your device. Expo's handling of this data is governed by Expo's own privacy terms.
  • Other users: As described in the section above, certain information is shared with other participants as part of the App's social features.
  • Legal reasons: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Toil, LLC, our users, or others.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the App: open Profile and choose Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no grace period, and a deleted account cannot be recovered.

What deletion removes

  • Your profile, including your display name and avatar
  • Your email address
  • Your notification settings and any stored device push tokens
  • Your friendships and any pending friend requests
  • Any challenge invitations you have sent or received
  • Your sign-in credentials
  • Challenges you created that no one else joined

What deletion does not remove

Some information remains, because removing it would alter other people's records rather than only your own. In each case your name is replaced with "Deleted user" and the information is no longer connected to you:

  • Activities you logged in challenges shared with others, so that those participants' totals and final results stay accurate.
  • Challenges you created that other people joined, so those participants keep access to them.
  • Messages you posted on challenge encouragement boards, so the conversations others took part in remain readable.

If you have uninstalled the App

You can still request deletion. Email us at friendlychallengeteam@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, and we will delete it for you. See our account deletion page for full details.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Access to data is restricted by server-enforced security rules, so that you can only modify records belonging to you, and results that have been finalised cannot be altered from a device. Your personal details — your email address, notification settings, subscription status and push notification tokens — are readable only by your own account.

Please note that this is a different question from what is visible to other users. See "Information Visible to Other Users" above for what other people can see.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this Policy. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
