Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 1, 2026

You can delete your Friendly Challenge account and its associated personal data at any time. This page explains how to do it, and exactly what is and is not removed.

If you still have the App installed

This is the fastest route, and it does not require contacting us.

  • Open Friendly Challenge and sign in
  • Go to the Profile tab
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  • Confirm when prompted

Deletion happens immediately. It is permanent — there is no grace period, and a deleted account cannot be recovered.

If you have already uninstalled the App

Email us at friendlychallengeteam@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm once it is done. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder before acting on it.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, including your display name and avatar
  • Your email address
  • Your notification settings and any stored device push tokens
  • Your friendships and any pending friend requests
  • Any challenge invitations you have sent or received
  • Your sign-in credentials
  • Challenges you created that no one else joined

What is kept, and why

Friendly Challenge is a shared activity app, so some of what you did forms part of other people's records as well as your own. Deleting it outright would change their results, not just yours.

The following remain, with your name replaced by "Deleted user" and no longer connected to you:

  • Activities you logged in challenges shared with others, so that those participants' totals and final results stay accurate.
  • Challenges you created that other people joined, so those participants keep access to them.
  • Messages you posted on challenge encouragement boards, so the conversations others took part in remain readable.

None of this retained information identifies you. Your display name, email address and profile are removed.

Timing

Data listed as deleted is removed at the time you delete your account; it is not held for a retention period first. The anonymised activity and challenge records described above are kept for as long as the challenges they belong to exist.
