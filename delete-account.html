Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 3, 2026

You can delete your Friendly Challenge account and its associated personal data at any time. This page explains how to do it, and exactly what is and is not removed.

If you still have the App installed

This is the fastest route, and it does not require contacting us.

  • Open Friendly Challenge and sign in
  • Go to the Profile tab
  • Tap Settings to open it, then scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap Delete account
  • Confirm, then type DELETE and confirm again

Deletion happens immediately. It is permanent — there is no grace period, and a deleted account cannot be recovered.

If you have already uninstalled the App

Email us at friendlychallengeteam@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm once it is done. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder before acting on it.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: your display name, username and avatar
  • Your email address and your sign-in credentials
  • Your account settings: notification settings, device push tokens, profile visibility, subscription status, and the record of when you accepted the Terms
  • Your membership in every challenge you were in
  • Challenges you created that no one else joined
  • The free-text notes you added to your activities
  • Your friendships, and friend requests you sent or received
  • Challenge invitations you sent or received, and requests you made to join a team
  • Custom activity types you created
  • Notifications sent to you
  • Feedback reports you sent from the App
  • Your session pings (the daily record of the days you opened the App)

What is kept, and why

Friendly Challenge is a shared activity app, so some of what you did forms part of other people's records as well as your own. Deleting it outright would change their results, not just yours.

The following remain. Where it is kept, your name is replaced with "Deleted user" and it is no longer connected to your account:

  • The values of the activities you logged: the activity type, amount, unit and date, and the challenge each one counted toward, with no name and no note. Other participants' totals, standings and final results were calculated from them, so removing them would change those results. This applies to all of your activities, including any in challenges only you took part in.
  • Your likes and comments on other people's activities, with your name removed. A comment's text stays, so the conversation on that activity still reads.
  • Messages you posted on challenge encouragement boards, with your name removed, so the conversations others took part in remain readable.
  • Your place in the final results of completed challenges, with your name replaced, so other participants' standings do not change.
  • Challenges you created that other people joined, without you as their creator, so those participants keep them.
  • The text of notifications other people already received. A notification such as "Sam won the challenge!" stays in its recipients' notification lists as it was written, because it is their record of what happened. The account link on it is removed. Each recipient can delete their own notifications.
  • A record that your account was deleted: your account's internal identifier, when the deletion was requested and completed, and which steps ran. We keep it so that a deletion interrupted partway can be finished, and as a record that your request was carried out. It holds no name, email address or content.

Apart from the text of notifications other people already received, none of this identifies you. Your display name, username, email address and profile are removed.

Timing

Data listed as deleted is removed at the time you delete your account; it is not held for a retention period first. A request by email is carried out once we have confirmed it comes from the account holder. What is kept is not deleted later: it stays for as long as the records it belongs to exist.
